Fictional title: Combined Maintenance Signals and Synthetic Outcome R

Article type: Original Research.

Problem: Separate maintenance indicators provide inconsistent warnings when equipment stress occurs as a combined pattern.

Design: A fictional longitudinal equipment dataset compared individual indicators with a prespecified joint-pattern analysis. No intervention was tested.

Findings: The joint stress pattern preceded Synthetic Outcome R more consistently than any individual indicator. Results varied by equipment class, and no formal between-class difference was established.

Implication: The result supports prospective validation of combined indicators for maintenance planning; it does not establish that changing the indicators prevents failure.

Confirmed declarations: original work and author approval.
