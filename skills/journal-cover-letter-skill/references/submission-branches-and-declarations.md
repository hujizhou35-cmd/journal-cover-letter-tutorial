# Submission branches and declarations v3.0

Classify the branch before drafting: initial submission, revision/resubmission, transfer, invited submission, or other/unresolved. Do not reuse language from another branch.

Record separately:

- `official_article_type`: exact journal submission label;
- `intellectual_route`: the reasoning route used to write the letter.

For an initial submission, verify originality, exclusive consideration, author approval, conflicts, funding, ethics/consent where applicable, preprint status, data/code availability when requested, and AI-use disclosure when required.

Verification and inclusion are separate decisions. A declaration belongs in the final cover letter only when required, standard and decision-relevant, requested by the user, or necessary to disclose an unusual circumstance.

Do not automatically insert funding, ethics, consent, data/code, preprint, or AI-use statements merely because they were verified. Never invent or assume declarations. Missing mandatory declarations require `NEEDS_AUTHOR_CONFIRMATION`.
