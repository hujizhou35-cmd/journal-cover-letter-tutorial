# Submission branches and declarations

Classify the branch before drafting: initial submission, revision/resubmission, transfer, invited submission, or other/unresolved. Do not reuse language from another branch.

For an initial submission, verify originality, exclusive consideration, author approval, conflicts, funding, ethics/consent where applicable, preprint status, data/code availability when requested, and AI-use disclosure when required.

Verification and inclusion are separate decisions. A declaration belongs in the final cover letter only when:

1. the journal or publisher explicitly requires it in the cover letter;
2. it is standard and decision-relevant for the submission branch, such as originality, exclusive consideration, author approval, and competing interests;
3. the user requests it; or
4. an unusual circumstance must be disclosed to the editor.

Do not automatically insert funding, ethics, consent, data/code, preprint, or AI-use statements merely because they were verified during intake. These may belong in the manuscript or submission system instead.

For a revision, identify the manuscript number and revision stage. Do not duplicate the point-by-point response in the cover letter. For a transfer, verify what metadata and declarations carry over and what the new journal requires.

Never invent or assume declarations. Missing mandatory declarations require `NEEDS_AUTHOR_CONFIRMATION`.
