# Journal Cover Letter Skill v2.0 (Legacy)

Version 2.0 gives Original Research and Reviews different writing logic. Research often needs one discovery story; a Review does not have to imitate that story and instead explains what becomes clearer when evidence is brought together. The language is more persuasive while scientific claims remain checked.

v2.0 为 Original Research 和 Review 采用不同逻辑。Research 往往需要一条发现故事；Review 不必模仿这条故事线，而应说明综合证据后出现了什么新理解。语言更有吸引力，但科学论断仍需严格核对。

Kept for comparison. New users should normally choose v2.1.

本版本用于比较；新用户通常应选择 v2.1。
